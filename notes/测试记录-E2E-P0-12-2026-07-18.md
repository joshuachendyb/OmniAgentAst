2. **Tool Router** — Determines whether a direct tool exists (`calendar`, `shell`, `grep`, etc.) or requires `searchtool` to discover an unloaded category.
3. **Execution Engine** — Runs the selected tool(s) with validated parameters, captures outputs, and feeds results back into the reasoning loop.
4. **Reasoning Loop** — Iteratively plans → executes → verifies → adapts until the task objective is met.

**How Streaming Works**:
- The LLM generates token-by-token in a continuous stream rather than waiting for a complete response.
- Each token is transmitted to the client incrementally, reducing perceived latency.
- Tool calls are embedded within the streamed text as structured markers. When a tool call is detected, execution pauses the text stream, the tool runs asynchronously, results are injected, and the stream resumes.
